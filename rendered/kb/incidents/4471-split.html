The two problems

parser: ticket #1. The shared parser reads every date month first, and these tenants write the day first. The dates come out flipped. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: ticket #2. Under its date field, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type month first as told, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. These reports come only through the form, from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix did not change this. Won’t fix: engineering retired the booking-v3 form.

The fix did not resolve the hint reports. The problem ends only because the form is retired, so do not count these reports as fixed by the parser change.

Look up the tenant, then ask how the report came in. The subject does not help, because both causes share subject lines. Neither does the intake tag: it is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and every en-US browser was tagged hint, whatever the tenant runs.
